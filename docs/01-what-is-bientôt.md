# What is Bientôt?

## Introduction

Every night, I make a plan. Ten things, neatly listed, each one sure it will fit.

By evening, three are done. Sometimes none. The rest roll over to tomorrow and join tomorrow's ten, and the list keeps growing while the day stays the same size.

For a long time, I thought the problem was me: not enough discipline, not enough focus, not enough hours. But the pressure didn't come from the work. It came from the list. A plan that can't fit in a day doesn't motivate you. It hurries you, and a hurried mind does slow, scattered work. My plans weren't helping me get things done. They were getting in the way.

The truth was simple and a little embarrassing: **I don't know how long things take.** I say "five minutes" and mean forty. I say "soon" and mean never. And I'm not alone. Almost everyone plans the day they _wish_ they had, not the day they'll _actually_ have.

So I decided to build the planner I needed.

**Bientôt** (French for _soon_) is a planner that learns how _you_ misjudge time. Each task you finish teaches it a little more: which kinds of work you underestimate, by how much, and when. Over time, it starts predicting how long your tasks will really take. It tells you when you've planned too much, flags a day heavy enough to exhaust you, and helps you build a plan you can actually finish.

It doesn't ask you to plan better. It quietly learns, so your plans become honest.

_Plan the day you'll actually have._

## Who it's for

Bientôt is for anyone whose to-do list is longer than their day.

- **The overplanner** writes ten tasks every morning, finishes three, and goes to bed feeling behind.
- **The student** is sure the assignment will take an hour. Then it takes the whole evening.
- **The working professional** has a calendar full of "quick" tasks that are never quick.
- **The freelancer or self-learner** has no manager setting deadlines, only their own optimistic guesses.
- **Anyone who feels constantly rushed**, not because there's too much to do, but because they planned as if there were more time than there is.

What they share isn't a job or an age. It's a habit: **trusting their first guess about time.**

Bientôt is built for one person and their own patterns. It isn't a team tool. It doesn't compare you to anyone else, and it doesn't care how productive other people are. It only learns how _you_ work, so it can help _you_ plan.

## How it works

Bientôt works in a simple loop. The more you use it, the better it knows you.

### 1. You plan

You add a task, give it a label and a category, and guess how long it will take. That's the same guess you'd make anywhere else.

### 2. Bientôt checks your guess

If it has learned that you usually take longer on this kind of task, it suggests a better estimate. You can accept it or keep your own. Either way, your original guess is saved.

### 3. You do the work

When you finish, you record how long it actually took, and if it ran over, why: it was genuinely hard, you got distracted, or something came up. Then you note how you feel after it.

### 4. Bientôt learns

Every gap between your guess and reality becomes a lesson. The first seven days are a quiet learning period: Bientôt watches and doesn't predict yet.

### 5. Bientôt predicts

After that, every new task gets a prediction and a simple verdict:

- _You need more time_
- _Your estimate is about right_
- _This might take less than you think_

### 6. Bientôt protects your day

When you plan a whole day, it checks three things: whether the tasks fit your hours, whether there are more than you usually finish, and whether the load is healthy. If not, it tells you gently and suggests moving something to tomorrow.

Then the loop begins again, and each time, the plan gets a little more honest.

## Features

1. **Sign up & onboarding:** create an account and set your usual daily hours.
2. **Add tasks:** a label, a category, your estimate and a planned date.
3. **Manage tasks:** edit, delete, and view tasks by date or status.
4. **Estimate suggestion:** a better estimate when your guess is off by more than 15%.
5. **Prediction & verdict:** the predicted duration, with _need more_, _about right_ or _might take less_.
6. **Fallback prediction:** estimate × category average whenever the model is down.
7. **Prediction history:** every prediction saved next to the actual time.
8. **Overrun reasons:** why a task ran long (genuinely long, distracted, something came up).
9. **Feeling after a task:** one tap for how you feel after finishing.
10. **Daily outcomes:** done, not done or moved, which gives your real completion rate.
11. **Plan my day:** checks whether your tasks fit your hours.
12. **The three checks:** time, task count and load.
13. **Healthy limits:** flags too much deep focus or learning in a day.
14. **Dashboard:** accuracy, completion rate and your progress.
15. **Where your time goes:** your hours broken down by category.
16. **When you perform best** _(v2)_: your best times and conditions for work.

## What makes it different

Most planners trust you. Bientôt checks your guesses.

| Most planners                   | Bientôt                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Take your estimate as fact      | Learns how far off your estimates usually are                   |
| Treat every user the same       | Learns _your_ patterns, per category                            |
| Only track whether you finished | Also tracks how long it took, why it ran over, and how you felt |
| Let you plan an impossible day  | Warns you before the day starts                                 |
| Measure productivity            | Also look out for your wellbeing                                |
| Ask you to change               | Adjust to you                                                   |

**In one line:** other planners help you organise your tasks, while Bientôt helps you understand your time.

## What it's not

Bientôt does one thing well, so it deliberately leaves out these:

- **Not a calendar.** It plans tasks, not meetings or events.
- **Not a team tool.** It's built for one person and their own patterns.
- **Not a timer app.** It doesn't run pomodoros or track you live.
- **Not a productivity score.** It doesn't rank you or compare you to anyone.
- **Not a habit tracker.** It learns how long your work takes, not whether you keep streaks.

## How to make the most of it

1. **Be honest with your estimates.** Guess the way you naturally would. Bientôt learns from your real habits, not your best behaviour.
2. **Log the actual time every time.** Each finished task is a lesson, and skipped tasks teach nothing.
3. **Give the reason when a task runs over.** "Distracted" and "genuinely long" teach Bientôt very different things.
4. **Tap how you feel.** It takes one second and is what makes healthy limits personal.
5. **Be patient for the first week.** Predictions start after seven days of learning.
6. **Use the same labels.** "DBMS assignment" every time, not "dbms hw" one day and "database work" the next.
7. **Plan your day in Bientôt.** The three checks only help if your plan is in there.
8. **Close the day.** Mark tasks done, not done or moved, so your completion rate stays true.
9. **Listen to the warnings.** If it says the day won't fit, move something before you start, not at 10 pm.

## Glossary

| Term                 | Meaning                                                                               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Task**             | One piece of work you plan to do                                                      |
| **Label**            | Your own name for a task, e.g. _"DBMS assignment"_                                    |
| **Base category**    | The fixed group a label belongs to, e.g. _Study_, _Work_, _Chores_                    |
| **Estimate**         | Your guess of how long a task will take                                               |
| **Actual**           | How long it really took                                                               |
| **Suggestion**       | Bientôt's better estimate, shown when yours is more than 15% off                      |
| **Prediction**       | Bientôt's forecast of how long a task will really take                                |
| **Verdict**          | The one-line summary of a prediction: _need more_, _about right_ or _might take less_ |
| **Factor**           | How much longer or shorter you usually take, e.g. 1.3 = 30% longer                    |
| **Cold period**      | Your first 7 days, when Bientôt learns before it predicts                             |
| **Fallback**         | The backup prediction (estimate × category factor) used when the model is down        |
| **Overrun reason**   | Why a task took longer: genuinely long, distracted, or something came up              |
| **Feeling**          | Your one-tap rating of how you feel after a task                                      |
| **Outcome**          | A task's end-of-day status: done, not done or moved                                   |
| **Completion rate**  | How many of your planned tasks you usually finish                                     |
| **Load**             | How heavy a day is, especially in deep focus or learning                              |
| **Healthy limit**    | The most load that's good for you in a day                                            |
| **The three checks** | Time, count and load, run on every planned day                                        |
